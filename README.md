## 💼 What I've Worked On

### 1. Enterprise Automation & Backend Pipelines — Sapi Technologies LLC
* **Role**: Software Engineering Intern
* **What I Engineered**:
  * Architected **SAPI_WATCHER**, a multi-service order processing pipeline that automatically captures customer orders from communication channels and routes them straight into production enterprise systems.
  * Built a Node.js listener and Python Flask backend that ingests incoming WhatsApp orders and parses attached PDF purchase invoices using PyMuPDF.
  * Implemented fuzzy NLP matching algorithms to map messy, conversational customer text to master enterprise inventory catalogs.
  * Developed a desktop administration dashboard in CustomTkinter featuring QR-code auth, live streaming logs, and human-in-the-loop verification modals.
  * Configured ODBC synchronization to remote Microsoft SQL Server ERP databases, pushing verified orders and syncing tiered client pricing tables in real time.

### 2. Mechanical Airflow & HVAC Duct Design — New Results Heating & Cooling
* **Role**: HVAC Systems & Air Duct Designer
* **What I Engineered**:
  * Designed complete supply and return air distribution systems for newly built custom homes across Central Texas.
  * Performed room-by-room heating/cooling CFM airflow calculations and external static pressure (ESP) balancing following ACCA Manual D and Manual J engineering standards.
  * Interpreted complex architectural blueprints and structural framing plans to route trunk-and-branch duct layouts that avoid load-bearing beams, joists, and plumbing runs.
  * Collaborated directly with architects, general contractors, and on-site tradesmen to ensure precise physical tolerances and high energy efficiency ratings.

* An admin-curated community job directory designed to help high school and teenage job seekers (ages 14–19) in the Greater Austin area find vetted, flexible employment opportunities. Features an interactive filterable job board, direct career application links, and an authenticated administrative dashboard for manual job creation and logo uploads.
